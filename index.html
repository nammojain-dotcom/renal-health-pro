Comprehensive Clinical Review Modules

Glomerular pathologies primarily damage the podocytes and basement membranes. Nephrotic syndrome is characterized by heavy proteinuria (>3.5g/24h), severe widespread swelling (anasarca), hypoalbuminemia, and hyperlipidemia. Major entities include Minimal Change Disease (MCD) and Focal Segmental Glomerulosclerosis (FSGS). Nephritic syndrome involves active inflammation of glomeruli, dysmorphic RBCs, hematuria, and hypertension (e.g., Glomerulonephritis, Lupus Nephritis).

In progressive parenchymal loss (CKD, Diabetic Nephropathy, Hypertensive Nephrosclerosis), the primary standard of care centers on controlling intraglomerular hydrostatic pressure. ACE inhibitors / ARBs selectively dilate efferent arterioles, dropping intraglomerular pressure and reducing proteinuria. SGLT2 inhibitors act via tubuloglomerular feedback to induce afferent arteriolar vasoconstriction, providing synergistic cardiorenal protection.

Complementary medical frameworks target metabolic waste clearing (*Aama*) and systemic fluid balance. Ayurvedic protocols deploy *Rasayana* herbs (*Punarnava*, *Gokshura*, *Varuna*) and *Basti* detox, while Unani remedies utilize stone-disintegrating (*Mofattet-e-Ashmari*) formulations like *Hajrul Yahood Bhasma*.
